ABOUT ADVENTURE ESCAPE SA

South African adventures, made easier to find

We bring outdoor experiences and professionally guided adventures together to help families, visitors, schools, and teams plan their next escape.

Explore experiences Plan your adventure

OUR STORY

A simpler way to plan an outdoor escape

Adventure Escape SA was founded by Liam Daniels after he noticed that families, tourists, schools, and corporate groups often had to search in many places to compare outdoor activities. The idea was to create one destination where people can discover experiences and plan their next adventure.

We connect guests with professionally guided activities across South Africa. Safety, enjoyable experiences, sustainable tourism, and partnerships with local guides and businesses are central to the way we want to grow.

WHY WE'RE HERE

Our Mission

Make it easier to discover and book outdoor activities, while inspiring people to explore South Africa with confidence.

WHERE WE'RE GOING

Our Vision

Become a trusted adventure-booking destination, known for quality, customer care, and a lasting respect for nature.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Approach

Put guest safety first, work with local guides and businesses, and help create memorable experiences in the outdoors.
